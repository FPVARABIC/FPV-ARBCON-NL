## Betaflight

Layout adaptations (workbench panels, setup stage/instrument arrangement, desktop serial-port table, tuning subtabs and motor-control columns) were informed by Betaflight App source at commit `568ac79a900356d3f2f117f1b90d4cf48fe424e3`:
https://github.com/betaflight/betaflight-configurator/tree/568ac79a900356d3f2f117f1b90d4cf48fe424e3

Copyright Betaflight contributors; GPL-3.0-or-later. Adapted for React Native/Web, Dutch labels, fiercon navy/gold identity, existing configuration controllers and event-driven pose updates. fiercon is an independent application and does not claim Betaflight endorsement.

The glTF aircraft in `src/ui/orientation3d/models` originate from that repository's `resources/models`: quad_x, tricopter, y6, y4, hex_plus, hex_x, quad_atail, quad_vtail and airplane. Rotary models retain upstream GPL provenance. Simplified `.mesh.json` derivatives are generated by `scripts/models/build_native_meshes.py` for the native renderer and browsers without WebGL; materials, scale and coordinate basis were adapted. The glTF preferred source is included. Additional unsupported mixer topologies are authored by fiercon from verified rotor layouts.

The airplane is **Low-Poly Biplane** by **lord_syrup**, CC BY 4.0:
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-biplane-755175daea384176813e7dc90b2245a5
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Its original credit is preserved in `public/licenses/airplane-CC-BY-4.0.txt` and beside the model. Modifications: coordinate conversion, centering, material adaptation and mesh simplification.

https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Source SHA-256: `9e0729ee253ca7d7a5c4ae9395fb1902264c5377c52e224d13dd85010e2835d9`. Converted to rounded equirectangular SVG paths, retaining polygon holes. License: `public/licenses/Natural-Earth-public-domain.txt`. No remote tiles or coordinate transmission are used for the embedded map.
